About Me

I am an electrical engineering student at Georgia Tech through a dual-degree program with Emory University, where I studied applied math and physics. What drew me to electrical engineering is the electrical grid. I want to learn about, and eventually help solve, the challenges of the energy transition, from energy storage to modeling how the grid behaves as it changes.

My long-term goal is to work in the energy industry on these problems. I hope to bring my background in math and scientific computing into electrical engineering, building simulations and models of the power grid that show where it can be improved. I am currently looking for a Summer 2027 internship in the energy industry, and I am always happy to connect with people who work in the field or are passionate about it.
